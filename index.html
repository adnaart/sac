Design thinking

Sac borrows the gallery of 1900: a gilded frame, brass fittings, a catalogue typed on cream card. Every control is a small object you can almost feel, from the lamp switch that glows while the film runs to the dial that clicks between three speeds.

Collecting is the heart of it. Pressing the bag lifts the frame on screen into a numbered plate, so watching becomes gathering.
